Who it is for

For people who need more than generic job-search advice.

Career Access is being designed around real employment barriers, including disrupted careers, limited UK labour-market experience, caring responsibilities, low confidence, language barriers and the practical pressure of applying for work without support on hand.

What it does

Focused on the employment tasks people need most.

01

CV to vacancy matching

Paste a real job advert and compare it with your existing CV. See where your experience matches and where stronger evidence may be needed.

02

ATS-friendly CV tailoring

Tailor your CV around a real vacancy using relevant skills, evidence and terminology while keeping your genuine experience at the centre.

03

Application support

Work through cover letters, supporting statements and application questions with guidance linked to the role you are applying for.

04

Interview preparation

Practise questions based on the actual vacancy, build STAR examples and prepare for the conversation before the real interview.

05

Employment information

Find general employment information and trusted sources when questions come up. Career Access is being designed to use authoritative sources such as GOV.UK and ACAS where appropriate.

06

Signposting to human help

Some situations need a person, not an AI answer. Career Access is designed to help users identify when further professional, community or specialist support may be appropriate.

Human support still matters

Career Access supports Employment Advisers. It does not replace them.

Employment support often happens at a scheduled time. Vacancies, interview invitations and workplace questions do not.

Career Access is being developed to extend practical support between appointments while keeping clear boundaries around situations that need human judgement, safeguarding, specialist help or professional advice.

In Somerset, Career Runway by CulturaLinks provides personalised employability support with CVs, applications, interviews and the move into work. Career Runway is the first real-world environment where Career Access is being tested and improved.

Get human support through Career Runway

Career Access provides general employment information and signposting. It is not a legal adviser and does not provide legal advice.

One programme. Two kinds of support.

Career Runway provides human employability support.

Career Access provides digital support when the next step cannot wait for the next appointment.
